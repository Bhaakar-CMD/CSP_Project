🌊 Surface Irrigation System

A traditional method that uses gravity flow for watering crops efficiently.

📖 Introduction

Surface irrigation is one of the oldest and most widely used irrigation methods. It works by distributing water over the soil surface by gravity. It is suitable for almost all types of soils and crops where water availability is moderate and land is relatively flat.

🔍 Types of Surface Irrigation

  • Basin Irrigation – Water is applied to a flat area surrounded by bunds.
  • Furrow Irrigation – Water flows through small channels between crop rows.
  • Border Irrigation – Water flows between parallel ridges.
  • Flood Irrigation – Entire field is flooded for water distribution.

⚙️ Components of a Surface Irrigation System

  • Water Source – Canal, well, or reservoir.
  • Main Channel – Carries water from the source to the field.
  • Field Channels – Distribute water to different plots.
  • Control Structures – Gates and bunds to regulate flow.
  • Field Bunds – Small soil ridges to contain water within plots.

🔧 How to Set Up

  1. Ensure the field is leveled for uniform water flow.
  2. Create bunds and channels for directing water.
  3. Construct main and field channels connected to a water source.
  4. Install gates or simple barriers to control water flow.
  5. Test water flow to avoid overflow or erosion.

▶️ How to Use

Open the water source and allow water to flow into the main channel. Control the flow using gates and direct it to different plots through field channels. Ensure even distribution and avoid waterlogging.

🛠️ Maintenance Tips

  • Regularly check bunds and channels for leaks or breaks.
  • Clean silt and debris from channels to maintain flow.
  • Repair erosion spots immediately.
  • Maintain proper slope for even distribution.
  • Avoid over-irrigation to prevent soil erosion and nutrient loss.
