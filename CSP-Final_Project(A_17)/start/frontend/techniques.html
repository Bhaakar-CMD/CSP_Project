💧 Modern Irrigation Techniques

Click on a method to learn setup, parts, usage, and maintenance.

1️⃣ Drip Irrigation 🌱

Efficient water delivery directly to plant roots.

2️⃣ Sprinkler Irrigation 💦

Sprays water like rainfall across fields.

3️⃣ Surface Irrigation 🌊

Traditional method using water flow over soil.

4️⃣ Subsurface Irrigation ⬇️

Delivers water beneath the soil surface.

5️⃣ Center Pivot 🚜

Rotating system watering large circular fields.

6️⃣ Furrow Irrigation 🌾

Water flows through small channels between rows.
