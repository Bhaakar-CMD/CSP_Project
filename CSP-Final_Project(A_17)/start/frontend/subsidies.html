💧 Government Subsidies for Irrigation Methods

Helpful info on subsidies to help farmers save water and increase productivity.

1. Drip Irrigation 💧

Subsidy Percentage: Around 55-60% of the system cost (higher for small and marginal farmers).

Land Area Eligibility: Usually available for farms starting from 1 acre and above.

How to Apply:

  • Contact your nearest Agriculture Department office or visit the PMKSY website.
  • Fill out the application form for the “Per Drop More Crop” scheme.
  • Submit land documents and proof of identity.
  • After approval, you will get subsidy support to install drip irrigation systems.

2. Sprinkler Irrigation 🚿

Subsidy Percentage: Typically 50-65% depending on farmer category and region.

Land Area Eligibility: Mostly for farms of 2 acres or more, varies by state.

How to Apply:

  • Visit your local Agricultural or Horticultural Department.
  • Apply under PMKSY or relevant state subsidy programs.
  • Provide necessary documents like land ownership proof.
  • After approval, you can purchase sprinklers with subsidy support.

3. Center Pivot Irrigation 🔄

Subsidy Percentage: Varies; usually subsidies come through Custom Hiring Centers or mechanization schemes.

Land Area Eligibility: Large farms (10+ acres) usually benefit most.

How to Apply:

  • Contact your State Agricultural Mechanization Department.
  • Look for Custom Hiring Centers (CHCs) offering center pivot equipment on rent/subsidy.
  • Submit farm size and identity documents.
  • Get access to subsidized rental/purchase options.

4. Surface Irrigation (Canal and Channel Improvement) 🌊

Subsidy Percentage: Not for irrigation systems but for infrastructure improvements like canal lining.

Land Area Eligibility: Depends on canal command areas.

How to Apply:

  • Approach local Water Resources or Irrigation Department.
  • Participate in canal lining/rehabilitation programs.
  • Usually coordinated by government agencies for village or cluster level projects.

5. Subsurface Irrigation 🌱

Subsidy Percentage: Limited subsidies, mainly pilot projects or research grants.

Land Area Eligibility: No large-scale subsidy programs yet.

How to Apply:

  • Check with agricultural research institutes or state agriculture departments.
  • Look for precision farming or R&D grant programs.
  • Mostly for experimental or commercial farms.
